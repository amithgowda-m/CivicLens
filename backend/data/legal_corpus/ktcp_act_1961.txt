[STATUTE]: Karnataka Town and Country Planning Act 1961
[ACT_NUMBER]: Act No. 11 of 1963

[SECTION 14]: Enforcement of Master Plan and Regulations
(1) On and from the date on which a Master Plan comes into operation, every land use, change of land use, and development of land within the planning area shall conform to the provisions of such Master Plan and the planning regulations attached thereto.
(2) Building setback regulations, road widening reserves, and floor area ratio (FAR) restrictions shall be strictly enforced by the planning authority. No development permission shall be granted for any commercial or residential building that fails to maintain the mandatory front setback of not less than 3.0 meters for plot widths exceeding 12 meters.

[SECTION 14-A]: Change of Land Use from Master Plan
(1) The Planning Authority may, with the previous sanction of the State Government, allow the change of land use from the Master Plan from Residential to Commercial or Industrial, provided that a public notice inviting objections and suggestions is published in at least two local newspapers giving a minimum window of 15 days for public submission.
(2) No change of land use shall be sanctioned within designated eco-sensitive lake buffer zones, natural drains (rajakaluves), or green belt reserves as specified in the Master Plan.

[SECTION 17]: Sanction of Layouts and Sub-divisions
(1) Every person intending to sub-divide or lay out land into building plots shall submit a plan to the Planning Authority.
(2) Mandatory open space reservations for parks and civic amenities shall be not less than 15 percent of the total layout area.
