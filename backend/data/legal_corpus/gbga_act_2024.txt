[STATUTE]: Greater Bengaluru Governance Act 2024
[ACT_NUMBER]: Karnataka Act No. 18 of 2024

[SECTION 108]: Property Tax and Municipal Surcharge Revision
(1) The Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) and Zonal Municipal Corporations may revise property tax assessment bands and commercial conversion surcharges up to a maximum limit of 20 percent per triennial cycle.
(2) Any revised surcharge levied on non-residential land conversions shall be allocated directly to ward-level infrastructure development funds under the supervision of the respective Ward Committee.

[SECTION 112]: Mandatory Ward Committee Public Consultations
(1) Ward Committees shall meet at least once every month to review municipal projects, infrastructure expenditure, and public objections regarding local zoning or civic works.
(2) All public notices regarding property tax revisions or zoning changes affecting a specific ward must be displayed on the ward office bulletin board and official online portal at least 15 calendar days prior to implementation.

[SECTION 145]: Lake Buffer Zone and Floodplain Regulations
(1) A strict buffer zone of 75 meters shall be maintained from the boundary of major lakes (Agara, Bellandur, Varthur) and 30 meters from primary storm water drains (rajakaluves).
(2) Construction of permanent commercial structures within designated lake buffer zones is strictly prohibited.
